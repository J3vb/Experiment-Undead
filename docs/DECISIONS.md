# Prototype decisions

The prototype follows the architecture doc
(https://claude.ai/artifact/XNJ12WAbMJAy2mRNPd5PCx). This file lists what is
built, what was simplified on purpose, and what is still to do.

## Built and matching the doc

- **Siphon (fixed Prototype, provisional):** the roster's gravity-well projectile is the one non-hitscan gun. Its provisional 10 m/s orb stops at the first surface or 25 m, then opens a 3 s well capturing at most 12 zombies over its lifetime and pulling only living targets within 6 m. The total per-zombie damage is roster 40 × 0.57 = 22.8 across the full duration, with no range or headshot bonus. The server owns pulls and damage and credits the claiming player; each player has at most two live wells. Recent launch positions keep landing claims valid while the shooter moves, and damage pulses send hit/kill confirmations to the owner. Its fixed `prototype` base has no Reforged variant or random affixes, and enters the Halcyon Labs Specimen Vault pool at weight 0.05; random drops and the future Fabricator implicit wait for their item-grant pipeline. The code-built Oriel model, viewmodel and generated energy pulse use the existing gun asset pipeline.

- **Equipped item stats (first runtime slice):** `ItemInstance` decodes saved roll fractions against current affix Resources into source-tagged modifiers; `StatBlock` adds increased values, multiplies more factors, adds flat values last and applies the seven limits in `content/loot/stat_caps.tres`. Gear feeds `Player.get_stat()` alongside the existing Serum multipliers, with health, regeneration delay and movement consumers. Gun timing/headshot hooks combine gear and gun buckets; damage, magazine, reserve, pellets, hip spread, range and recoil use independent per-run weapon/recoil copies. Item hits apply flat points and capped leech. Shared Resources and saved dictionaries stay unchanged. Vault and wall-ammo ownership compare stable weapon ids, including Reforged ids, so runtime copies remain recognized.
- **Run loadout bridge (provisional):** the local profile's preserved `loadout` field is a slot-to-item dictionary (`sidearm`, `primary`, `vest`, `charm`, `implant`); absent fields and the old empty-array placeholder keep the scene's starting loadout. The equipped pistol-class Sidearm replaces its start pistol; Vest, Charm and Implant apply to the player. The equipped Primary stays out at spawn: its round-5 Drop Locker is not built. Pack guns are not equipped during this slice. Gear affix content, equipment menus and loadout editing remain separate work. Traits, sets and `proc` lines retain their save data without running; contextual damage (Close quarters, Long shot, Elite hunter, Executioner) and stagger retain their stat values but have no runtime consumers in the permitted weapon hook yet.
- **Skinned zombies (provisional):** Shambler, Crawler and Bloater use continuous Skin/Subdivision bodies on the shared 22-bone character rig, connected torn coat shells, recessed mouths, detailed blank staff badges and mirrored hands with palm contact. Their dedicated texture-free zombie finish keeps matte skin, restrained cloth wear and a darker veined Belly; heads keep the lightest skin value, and only eyes and Belly glow bio-green. All paint zones remain main; the zombie shader has no dye or rarity inputs. The visual review at `docs/design/reviews/zombies-2026-11-15.md` records fresh before/after renders and hand/pose checks. Counts are 7,700 / 7,248 / 8,776 triangles and 8 / 7 / 8 materials; existing Shambler/Crawler 6,000–8,000 and Bloater 7,000–9,000 ranges stay unchanged (eight materials maximum). Crawler retains every bone but has no mesh weighted to leg bones. ZombieRig drives the procedural poses through bones, with grounded Crawler arm motion and the unchanged head BoneAttachment for the hit zone. Compatibility captures verify shape and value; Forward+ lighting and 24-enemy GPU performance still need validation.
- **Gear finishes and booth (provisional):** one texture-free, object-space `gear.gdshader` covers fabric, webbing, painted plate, polymer, rubber, knit and opaque lens. The character/gear importer selects finishes by Blender material name; AO stays in vertex red and alpha selects glow/trim/accent/main. `GearLook.apply()` sets per-instance dyes and rarity without changing shared materials; six `RarityLook` resources own trim, bounded glow and pulse/flicker. `tools/gear_booth.tscn` renders the player from four angles at 1280×720 under a warm key/rim and AgX; `--out <dir> --rarity all` also writes a sheet with one row per rarity. Its plain model array is the extension point for later gear; effects and appearance networking wait.
- **GearRig and LookBuilder (provisional):** `CharacterLook` holds filled vest/charm/implant visuals, real rarity, default main/accent dyes (`#5F6D68`, `#2F393B`) and set counts across all five equipped slots. Pure `LookBuilder.from_equipped()` maps item `base` and `rar`; unknown bases or visuals use the slot base mesh in Common. Item-base resources do not exist yet, so a fixed mapping covers the three vest frames, five charms and five implants; defaults are medium vest, dog tags and reflex implant. `GearRig` replaces bone-attached scenes, binds the vest Shell to the body skeleton and removes its first-person cuffs; implant Module and Port follow the left forearm and neck. The body follows synced yaw and its head follows pitch. With no idle clip, arms are lowered from the source A-pose; torso and legs remain at rest, with no walking animation. The owner sees only first person while its world body and gear cast shadows only; other players see the body normally. Solo match start reads the CharacterProfile slot-keyed `loadout` dictionary; missing or legacy empty-array loadouts remain empty. Appearance replication, first-person attachments, dyes, crests and effects remain separate steps. `tests/manual/gear_rig_look.tscn` captures the actual player with a Rare vest, Epic charm and Legendary implant in the existing booth; the image needs the person's eye.
- **Vest frames (provisional):** light is a flat chest rig, medium adds compact clavicle pads, and heavy adds broad pads and a tall collar. Each exports one Shell on the shared 22-bone rig, rigidly weighted world cuffs/pads, two unskinned first-person cuffs and a spine-mounted CharmClip. Source and exported geometry clear the full undersuit by at least 3 mm at rest. The task's light 3,000–4,500, medium 4,000–5,500 and heavy 4,500–6,000 triangle ranges replace the design document's provisional 3,000-triangle vest target. Four Gear finish names share the alpha-zone shader; trim covers at least 5%, glow at most 5% and only emits from Rare up. The booth shows the medium at six rarities or all three frames on the player body; GearRig now applies equipped swaps on the gameplay body.
- **Character and gear build tools (provisional):** shared 22-bone A-pose rig, deterministic segment weights and rigid binding; character/gear checks enforce budgets, registered stems, rig/weight rules and attachment origins. `Color` RGB keeps baked AO; alpha encodes main 1.0, accent 0.667, trim 0.333 and glow 0.0, with glow capped at 5% of surface area. The character registry includes the base player body, first-person arms and three vest frames; other gear meshes come in later batches.
- **Base player body (provisional):** `characters/player.glb` is a 1.80 m Lantern Post salvager undersuit on the shared 22-bone rig. One continuous Skin/Subdivision mesh carries the thermal suit and knitted balaclava, with carved seam channels; patches, goggles, gloves, boots and belt add sewn and rigid details. BodySuit, BodyKnit, BodyRubber and BodyLens select the four body finishes through the shared gear import. The task's 8,000–12,000 triangle range replaces the design document's 6,000-triangle body target. Broad-shouldered proportions, cold-weather tailoring and goggle tint are provisional; first-person arms and vest frames are separate builds described above and below.
- **First-person arms (model only):** `characters/player_arms.glb` keeps all 22 body bones in the same rest pose, with weights only on arm bones. The sleeves are cut from the body's surface halfway down the upper arms; shared glove palms, knit cuffs and knuckle pads gain stitched seams, wrist straps and fixed finger curls for a 32 mm right pistol grip and 45 mm left handguard. BodySuit, BodyRubber and BodyKnit keep the body's paint zones. The 5,000–8,000 triangle budget gives close views more detail; the shared gear importer selects matching body finishes, with dynamic lighting. Weapon fitting, render layers and gear attachments follow in a separate task.
- **Skill tree data and spending rules:** the 20 named design nodes load through Content, with provisional rows/columns and no prerequisite links specified yet. SkillTree checks free points, rank limits, prerequisites and branch gates without changing input ranks or content. No stat effects or Trainer screen yet; town respec limits and Scrap costs wait. Only the named nodes exist, so the 15/25-point gates cannot be reached until more nodes are added as data (normal ranks total 14 Marksman, 10 Survivor and 13 Tinkerer).
- **Weapon loot roller (first affix batch):** AffixData/AffixTier and RarityData Resources hold 22 weapon lines, three rarity-based implicit ammo lines and six rarity tables. ItemRoller uses only the supplied RNG for rarity/luck, counts, tag/family exclusions, gated tiers, roll fractions and ids, returning the versioned plain item dict; Legendary rolls four lines with a null trait, and Prototype weapon rolls are rejected. Equal-fifth tiers use a 0.1 step where their boundaries are fractional (otherwise 1). Gear content, traits, named Legendaries, sets, match drops and loot UI wait for later batches.
- **Godot 4.7.2, Forward+, Jolt at 60 Hz, physics interpolation on.**
- **Typed GDScript enforced:** `untyped_declaration` and `inferred_declaration` are errors (addons excluded).
- **Folders:** `core/` autoloads, `systems/<feature>/` code + scenes, `content/<type>/` data only.
- **Autoloads:** `Events` (past-tense facts only), `Content` (id = file name, strips `.remap`), `Net` (`is_authority()`, id allocation).
- **Content as Resources:** `WeaponData` + `RecoilData` (per-shot pattern), `EnemyData`, `WaveRules` + `EnemySpawnEntry`, `EconomyRules`. Live state is in `WeaponInstance`; `.tres` files are never mutated (tested).
- **Damage:** `DamageInfo` (amount, attacker, weapon id, type, zone, position, normal, impulse) → `HitZone` (Area3D on layer 4) → `HealthComponent`. Weapon head/limb multipliers from data, per-zone armour on the HitZone.
- **Collision layers 1-8** named as in the doc.
- **Multiplayer rules:**
  1. Player reads `InputFrame`s from a `LocalInput` node, never the keyboard.
  2. Damage, spawns, XP only apply where `Net.is_authority()`.
  3. Logic emits facts; muzzle flash, sound, impacts and hit markers react (weapon `fired`/`traced` signals).
  4. Gameplay in `_physics_process`; `GameSession.tick` counts ticks. Weapon `tick()` runs from the player's physics step; `_process` is visuals only.
  5. All enemies come from `Spawner`, which assigns `net_id`.
  6. Gameplay randomness uses `GameSession.rng` (seedable).
  7. XP goes through `ProgressionService` (one in-memory class for now).
  8. No global player: everything loops the `players` group.
- **Local character (P5):** one `solo` character until character selection in P6. `user://characters/<id>.json` stores id, display name, total XP and skill ranks (empty until skills); level comes from total XP. Unknown fields survive saves. Game over, exfil (after +50%) and leaving early bank run XP once; unreadable/newer files stay untouched and use a temporary character.
- **Economy:** per-player `Wallet`; `Economy` pays from `EconomyRules` and owns `try_spend()`.
- **XP rules and levels:** `XpRules` (`content/progression/xp_rules.tres`) holds kill/round rewards and the rounded level curve from the balance CSV. `ProgressionService` owns run XP plus a starting total from the local character, grants headshot and living-player round-clear XP, and emits every new level up to 50. Skill-point totals include the five bonus levels (54 at level 50); skills are not built yet. Exfil still bonuses only run XP.
- **Wave director:** intermission → `round_started` → spawning (max alive cap, extra per player) → clearing → `round_ended`.
- **Zombie AI:** enum state machine (Spawning, Entering, Chasing, Attacking, Dead), NavigationAgent3D with avoidance off plus cheap separation, re-path interval by distance (0.25 / 0.5 / 1.5 s), target = nearest living player every 0.5 s.
- **Fire timing:** time accumulator plus a 0.12 s input buffer, semi / burst / auto.
- **Look:** volumetric fog, depth fog, AgX, SSAO, glow, flickering shadowed lamps, moonlight, player flashlight.
- **Look reference (Cold Containment):** `levels/look_reference/halcyon_corridor.tscn` rebuilds the style guide's Halcyon Labs corridor in Godot. Its `WorldEnvironment` points at the shared `levels/looks/halcyon_interior.tres`; interior game scenes must use this file, not a copy. Render it with `tools/look_shot.gd` (command in the file). Differences from the guide's table, on purpose: AgX instead of ACES; ambient energy 0.2 instead of 0.35 (baked bounce now fills the shadows); fog albedo is a light teal-grey because Godot's fog albedo is the scatter colour (the guide's #0A1416 was the web renderer's fog colour). Grain and vignette come from `systems/effects/film_post.tscn` (a CanvasLayer any scene can add). Zombies there are the code-built Shambler, Crawler and Bloater models (static, no game logic), and no light flickers so renders stay comparable. Style-guide light budget: at most 6 shadowed real-time lights in view (tested). Surface detail is procedural and world-space, no texture files: `levels/materials/grime.gdshader` (wall drips, floor-level dirt, ceiling stains; the wall panel's glTF materials are remapped to it in its `.import` file) and `wet_tiles.gdshader` (tiles, grout, mirror puddles for SSR). Fixtures have a dark housing; one unshadowed `ZombieRim` spot behind the zombies shines back toward the player so silhouettes read. Bounce light is baked with LightmapGI (High, 3 bounces, denoiser, texel scale 2; about 2.3 MB in `halcyon_corridor.exr` + `.lmbake`, plain git). Fixtures and the bio-leak glow use bake mode Dynamic: direct light and shadows stay real-time, only their bounce is baked. `ZombieRim` and the camera `ViewFill` are cheat lights with bake mode Disabled. Each fixture has a `CeilingGlow` omni (bake mode Static, no shadow, no fog) just under it, so the ceiling between fixtures gets the spill a real tube gives; it exists only in the bake and costs nothing at run time. Zombies and the rifle import with light baking Dynamic, so they are not baked and take light from the lightmap probes. The wall panel imports with Static Lightmaps (UV2, `.unwrap_cache` committed so UV2 is the same on every machine). `Block` builds its box at runtime as an internal child, which LightmapGI skips (it ignores unowned nodes), so `Block` hands the box over through `get_bake_meshes()` / `get_bake_mesh_instance()`, the hooks GridMap uses. **Re-bake after any geometry or light change:** Godot 4 has no command-line bake, so open the scene in the editor on a GPU, select `LightmapGI`, press Bake Lightmaps, save, and commit the new `.exr` and `.lmbake`.
- **Halcyon Labs (default map, blockout):** `levels/halcyon_labs/layout.json` holds rooms (rect, floor height, ceiling height), door cuts and ramps as plain data. `tools/blockout_builder.gd` turns it into `blockout.tscn` (command in the file; never hand-edit the output, a test fails if it is stale). Walls are runs of the code-built wall panel with one collision box per run; ceiling lights are the kit scene `levels/kit/ceiling_light.tscn`, two shadowed per room. `map.tscn` holds the gameplay nodes: player spawn, 21 `ZombieSpawnPoint`s (each tagged with its area), dock props, orange doors and green machine placeholders. `Level.open_area()` puts an area's spawn points into the Spawner's group; only the Loading Dock is open at the start. Closed doorways cut the navmesh (tested). Layout and costs follow the maps doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/UxyrXQqFfonMxh3MgFMYWY). The prototype courtyard is removed.
- **Halcyon Labs lightmaps:** the full map is baked with LightmapGI like the corridor (High, 3 bounces, denoiser), texel scale 1.5 instead of 2 so the files stay small for plain git: about 13 MB (`map.exr` 12.8 MB + `map.lmbake` 0.2 MB). The `LightmapGI` lives in `map.tscn`, not in the generated `blockout.tscn`; the builder now gives the wall strips above the panels UV2 so they bake. Baked: blockout floors, walls, ceilings, ramps, kit panels, dock props, Med Dispensers, Reforger, power switch, exfil hatch. Not baked, because they move or vanish and would leave their shadow in the lightmap: door blockers, wall-buy boards and the Specimen Vault body (`Block.baked = false`, lit by the probes), barrier planks (GI Dynamic), the vault glow (GI Disabled). In-game zombies set their meshes to GI Disabled at spawn: their limbs move every frame and Dynamic GI re-sampled the probes for each limb each frame, which cost about a quarter of the frame with 24 zombies (the corridor's static zombies keep the Dynamic import). Every `CeilingLight` Fixture is bake mode Dynamic; the Spill omni lights are deleted from the kit (the bake now gives that fill, and rooms keep more contrast). Power-off: the bounce is baked with the power-on colours, but it is faint enough that the emergency rooms still read dark red (screenshots matched before and after), so LightmapGI energy and the 0.2 ambient stay as they are. Window spawn spaces stay dark. At 1440p with 24 zombies the four test spots run 400 to 500 fps, the same as before the bake. **Re-bake after a layout change:** rebuild the blockout (`tools/blockout_builder.gd`), open `levels/halcyon_labs/map.tscn` in the editor on a GPU, select `LightmapGI`, press Bake Lightmaps, and commit only `map.exr`, `map.exr.import`, `map.lmbake` and `map.tscn` (revert anything else the editor re-saved, and keep `map.tscn` free of editor-only uids).
- **Interactables:** `Interactable` (Area3D on the interactable layer, `get_prompt()` + `interact()`); the player ray-casts 2.5 m from the camera each tick into `Player.focus`, presses interact, and the HUD shows the prompt. `Door` (cost, area, needs power; doors to the same area open together; opening frees its blocker and rebakes the navmesh at the end of the frame, one bake for any number of doors; the bake runs on the main thread because threaded bakes never finished on the 2-core CI runner, so a purchase costs one short hitch (move to per-area regions if it shows in profiling)), `PowerSwitch` (`Level.turn_power_on()`, free power doors open themselves), `WallBuy` (gun at its `WeaponData.cost`, ammo for half). All four Halcyon Labs wall guns are real WallBuys; no wall-gun placeholders remain. The map's NavigationMesh is local to scene, so a restarted match never shares a mesh that is still baking.
- **Serums and Med Dispensers:** `SerumData` (`content/serums/`) is a name, a cost and a dictionary of stat multipliers. `Player.get_stat()` multiplies the multipliers of every Serum the player holds; the gun reads `reload_time`, `fire_interval`, `ads_time` and `head_damage` through it, and the player applies `max_health` and `weapon_slots` when a Serum is bought. Equipment now shares StatBlock buckets with these multipliers, as described above. `MedDispenser` (`systems/interactables/med_dispenser.tscn`) sells one Serum, needs power (its strip lights up when the power comes on), once per run. All eight Serums are real: Ironhide, Quickhands, Overclock, Deadeye, Mule, Stride, Second Wind, Scavenger. Stride doubles `sprint_time` and halves `sprint_out_time` (the gun's sprint-to-fire delay); its "slide 25% further" waits for a slide. Prices are the design doc's base prices, like the doors (the balance sheet's provisional x2.5 is not applied). Provisional: all dispensers need power, no limit on how many Serums a player holds.
- **Reforger:** Tier I only. `Reforger` swaps the gun in hand for `WeaponData.upgraded` (a separate `*_reforged.tres`: damage x2, magazine +50%, reserve x2, checked by a content test) in the same slot with full ammo, for 5000. A gun with no further tier buys a full refill for 2500. Wall-buys treat the Reforged gun as owned and sell its ammo for 4000 (balance sheet, provisional). Instant swap, no waiting animation yet. Tiers II (ammo mods) and III come later.
- **Brenner Trench (third gun, wall-buy 750 in the Loading Dock, the start area, per the maps doc):** pump shotgun, 8 pellets, code-built model `shotgun_brenner.glb` (the wooden pump is the `Bolt` node, so the existing bolt kick racks it, and the left hand rides on it). Pellet damage is the balance sheet's 35 scaled by the same 0.57 the prototype AR uses (34 vs 60), so 20 x 8. Reload is one timed action for the whole tube (2.6 s, 3.6 s empty) instead of shell by shell; per-shell reload comes with real animations. Shared sounds except its own generated `shotgun_fire.wav`.
- **Kestrel SMG (wall-buy 1000 at the existing Halcyon Labs spot):** automatic, 850 rpm, 30 rounds and 180 reserve, 12–35 m falloff, 2.5x headshots; damage is balance-sheet 45 x 0.57 = 25.65, falling to 17 at range. Tier I Kestrel Reforged doubles damage and reserve (51.3/34, 360 reserve) with a 45-round magazine. Code-built compact model, folding wire stock, straight magazine, iron sights and its own short generated shot; shared dry-fire and reload sounds. Provisional feel: 3.0 hip spread, 0.2 bloom per shot, lighter vertical recoil with more lateral sway, 0.18 s ADS, 2.0/2.6 s reload. Wall ammo costs 500; also in the Specimen Vault pool.
- **Talon DMR (wall-buy 1500 in Generator Hall):** semi-auto marksman rifle, 300 rpm, 15-round box magazine, long braked barrel, fixed stock with cheek riser and a 3x scope. Damage is balance-sheet 120 x 0.57 = 68.4, falling to 55 at range; the 3.0 head multiplier (balance sheet issue 6) is provisional. A close headshot deals 205.2 against a round-one Walker's 100 health; a body shot (68.4) does not kill. Tight ADS, wide hip spread and one strong vertical kick with fast recovery. Talon Reforged doubles damage and reserve, with 22 rounds (+50%, truncated by the existing Tier I rule). Own generated firing crack and tail; shared reload sound.
- **Kestrel / Talon model detail:** both use the rifle/shotgun bevelled construction, separate bolt with charging handle, feed-lip magazine pivot, and gunmetal / matte-black / olive-grey polymer materials. Kestrel has a hinged skeleton stock and guarded iron sights; Talon has a long braked barrel, adjustable cheek rest, folded bipod and a non-emissive 3x optic. The Talon picture-in-picture lens mounts to the model eyepiece marker. Both reuse `Weapon` bolt/reload poses and import as Dynamic (light baking 3); tests cap each at 1.5x the rifle triangle count. Reproduce side and first-person asset comparison plates with `tools/render_weapon_comparison.py` (CPU Blender, using committed GLBs, content hip positions and 110-degree horizontal FOV).
- **Piercing rifles (provisional):** Fieldhand (`fieldhand_lever`) and Ironwood .50 (`ironwood_50`) use roster numbers with the existing damage ×0.57: 85.5 / 256.5, head ×3, and one / two extra zombies pierced. Each extra hit deals 80% of the previous hit; walls stop the ray and every zombie uses the ordinary hit path. The co-op claim budget includes pierced targets so normal firing cadence retains every hit. Fieldhand keeps full move speed and loads one round per 0.375 s (the roster’s 3 s divided across eight rounds); firing cancels loading. Brenner stays on its timed whole-tube reload. Fieldhand enters the Halcyon Vault; its Kessel Dam wall-buy waits for that map. Ironwood is loot only per this task, overriding the roster’s general every-base-in-Vault rule; Rare floor and unlock level 25 remain roster metadata until loot base selection exists. Both have Tier I Reforged resources, generated shot sounds (`tools/assets/piercing_sfx.py`), code-built models and viewmodels; Ironwood reuses the Corvid 4× picture-in-picture scope. `roster.csv` retains the historical designed/new classification while Build batch records B built / C built.
- **Sidearms (provisional Wren handling):** the start pistol (`pistol_service`, roster Warden P9) now uses the code-built `warden_p9.glb` in both base and Reforged content; every existing number and display name stays unchanged. Wren MP is an automatic machine pistol with a folding brace and 20-round extended magazine: roster damage 30 x 0.57 = 17.1 (10.26 at range), 1,000 rpm, 120 reserve, 1.6/2.0 s reload, 0.18 s ADS and full move speed. Its alternating recoil pattern follows the Kestrel with smaller kicks, less random spread and faster recovery. Tier I doubles damage/reserve and gives 30 rounds. Loot tags are `pistol;machine_pistol`; the Halcyon Specimen Vault includes it at weight 1.0. Long shot remains eligible for both Wren variants: shared affix data stays unchanged, so the roster's machine-pistol exclusion is deferred. Grauwald's 900-point wall buy and unlock-level-5 loot delivery wait for those systems/maps. Both models keep separate `Slide`, `Magazine`, `Trigger` and `Muzzle` parts; a `Bolt` parent lets the existing action animation drive the slide. Their viewmodel wrappers preserve the shared hands, sleeves and muzzle flashes. Wren has its own generated 0.12 s firing crack and mechanical snap, with shared dry-fire and reload sounds.
- **Vault guns, roster batch A1 (provisional numbers):** Hollis .44 (revolver, semi, 6 rounds), Mako PDW (3-round burst, 40 rounds), Atlas Auto (auto shotgun, 8 pellets, 10-shell box), Halberd LMG (auto, 100-round box) and Corvid .338 (bolt sniper, 5 rounds, 4x head, 4x scope). Only from the Specimen Vault, no wall. Sheet numbers from `weapons.csv` as they are, damage x 0.57 (Hollis 102.6, Mako 31.35, Atlas 17.1 x 8, Halberd 42.75, Corvid 342); falloff ranges, spread and recoil are new picks to match each role. `crate_weight` is the roster's drop weight / 100 (Hollis 1.0, Mako and Atlas 0.8, Halberd and Corvid 0.6), so heavy guns roll less often. Reforged versions follow Tier I. Each has its own code-built model (`tools/assets/<name>_model.py`, 4,684 to 6,900 triangles), viewmodel and generated shot sound. The revolver hammer is its `Bolt` and it has no `Magazine`, so reload is one timed action like the Brenner. The Corvid's bolt only kicks back like the other guns; no bolt-cycle animation yet. Atlas Auto keeps the sheet's DPS even though balance issue 5 flags it as too strong; that is a balance call for play-testing.
- **Loot rifles (roster batch B, provisional feel):** Sentry, Nightjar SD and Rampart BR have base/Reforged resources, loot tags and Halcyon Labs Vault entries. Roster damage x0.57 gives 39.9, 22.8 and 54.15; ammo, raw RPM, reload, ADS and movement match `roster.csv`. Sentry uses the existing three-round burst and fixed 0.12 s gap (the sheet's 0.6 RPM factor remains an estimate). Nightjar has 3x headshots, low recoil, an integral suppressor, a shorter/quieter generated shot and `no_muzzle_flash`, which suppresses both flash nodes locally and in co-op shot effects. Rampart retains its 1400 price for its future Marrow Junction wall buy; this batch adds no wall placement or stagger mechanic. Class-based falloff (65% minimum damage), empty reload times, spread and recoil are provisional. Unlock gating and actual match loot remain with the loot system.
- **Weapon visual review:** all nine guns have recessed actions, open vents/bores, restrained procedural wear and a thin vertex-masked rarity inlay, retaining original nodes and pivots. `tools/weapon_review.tscn` captures Common/Legendary booth, 10 m, hip and live ADS views; rarity tint is a review preview until runtime loot equips it. Existing budgets and material counts are unchanged. Scores and paired images: `docs/design/reviews/weapons-2026-11-15.md`.
- **Model turnarounds (provisional):** the `Turnarounds` workflow renders every `.glb` in `assets/models/` from front, 3/4, side and back (CPU Cycles, fixed lights, model scaled to fit) and builds a review sheet with the approved render, a diff (changed pixels in bio-green) and the category's style-guide image (`REFERENCES` in `tools/turnarounds.py`). It fails when more than 0.2% of pixels moved and `docs/design/turnarounds/` was not updated: check the sheets in the `turnarounds` artifact, then run `python tools/render_turnarounds.py --approve` and commit the new PNGs. A new model category needs a `REFERENCES` entry (a test checks it).
- **Specimen Vault (provisional rules):** one `SpecimenVault` per map, standing on one of the 6 `vault_spot` markers (starts on the nearest one, the Mess Hall on Halcyon Labs). Needs power, 950 a roll. A roll takes 2.5 s, picks from the map's `pool` (weighted by `WeaponData.crate_weight`, skipping guns the player holds as base or Reforged), then only the buyer can take the gun for 10 s; taking it works like a wall-buy (`WeaponHolder.give`, replaces the gun in hand when both slots are full). After 4 to 8 rolls (`GameSession.rng`) the last roll gives nothing, is refunded, and the Vault jumps to another spot. Halcyon's pool is the AR-7, Brenner Trench, Kestrel SMG, Talon DMR, Hollis .44, Mako PDW, Atlas Auto, Halberd LMG, Corvid .338, Sentry, Nightjar SD, Rampart BR, Fieldhand, Wren MP and Siphon; the Vault ignores unlock levels until loot exists. No rarity or affixes yet (loot phase), no roll animation beyond the label flicking through names, and the navmesh is not rebaked when the Vault moves (one 2 x 1 m box).
- **Power-ups:** `PowerUpData` (`content/power_ups/`) is a name; the effect is one `match` branch in `PowerUp.collect()`. `PowerUpDrops` (in the GameSession) rolls 2.5% per zombie kill with `GameSession.rng`, at most 4 drops a round, types from a shuffled deck. A drop floats 0.8 m above the kill for 25 s, blinks for the last 5 s, and the first living player to touch it collects it for every player. Only Resupply exists (full ammo for every gun of every player; no grenades yet). Blackout rounds and their guaranteed Resupply come later.
- **Exfil:** `ExfilHatch` (`systems/interactables/exfil_hatch.tscn`, Containment Wing floor) opens from round 10 every 5th round, for that round; its frame glows green and the HUD flashes "EXFIL OPEN". A player calls it with the interact key; the 60 s hold counts only while a living player is within 4 m (it pauses, not resets, when nobody is there; provisional). A called exfil stays open past the round end. When the hold finishes, `GameSession.finish_exfil()` adds +50% of the run's XP, freezes the level and shows the results with "Exfil successful". Bonus Scrap and the exfil cache come with loot (P6).
- **Scavenger:** no stat (in `SerumData.EFFECTS`). Each kill by its holder has a 10% chance (`PowerUpDrops.ammo_pack_chance`, provisional) to drop an ammo pack: the `ammo_pack` PowerUpData on the power-up pickup, not in the drop deck and not counted in the 4-a-round cap. Only the player who picks it up gets it: one magazine of reserve for every gun they hold. The "+1 grenade" part waits for grenades.
- **Second Wind (solo):** a Serum with no stat (listed in `SerumData.EFFECTS`; code checks `Player.has_serum()`). Lethal damage with Second Wind puts the player down for 3 s (no input, camera tilts, zombies ignore them because `is_alive()` is false), then back up with full health; Second Wind is used up and the other Serums stay. Its dispenser has `max_sales = 3` and disappears after the third sale. Without it, going down is death and the run ends. Co-op uses the step 4 rules below; Second Wind remains solo-only.
- **Sprint stamina:** 4 s of sprint (`Player.sprint_time`, scaled by the `sprint_time` stat). Running out blocks sprint for 3 s, then refills it; stopping early refills over 3 s (provisional). No stamina bar on the HUD.
- **Main menu (co-op step 6):** Play solo keeps the existing Halcyon Labs flow; Host and play opens a chosen port (default 7777), and Join accepts an address with an optional port. Both use the command-line launch scene flow. Settings remembers the last address and port. Joining shows the target with Cancel and a 6-second timeout; one themed dialog gives plain-word errors for bad addresses, unreachable servers, occupied ports, full servers, version/content mismatches and dropped connections, and distinguishes a host ending the match. SceneMultiplayer authentication carries rejection reasons before player admission, comparing the protocol, game version and a deterministic fingerprint of gameplay resource values. Host shutdown drains its reliable notice for up to 6 seconds so clients can receive it before the socket closes. Keyboard and gamepad focus covers the menu, fields and dialog. Shared Settings, bundled Credits, Quit, pause/results returns and the disabled Loading Dock backdrop remain. Continue, server browser, LAN discovery and character selection wait for their own phases.
- **Solo HUD and results:** anchored mockup layout with round flash/blink, exfil hold box, split ammo and prompt price, Serum code badges and health bar; observes Events and existing read-only presentation state. P5 adds a thin brass XP bar and level at the bottom edge (placement provisional), plus a fading level-up toast. Results use the mockup’s left stats and right Progress columns, replay saved total XP across level-ups and show skill points; Main menu has default focus, with Restart and R/Esc shortcuts. The middle loot column waits for P6 and View squad for P7. Headshots count killing headshots alongside kills in GameSession.

- **Assets:** binaries are in plain git (about 1 MB). No Git LFS for now (decided 2026-11-14); revisit when asset packs arrive, the lines to switch it on are in `.gitattributes`. - **Logging and diagnostics:** `Log` autoload (provisional: not in the architecture doc's autoload list) writes one JSON line per event to `user://logs/run_<time>_<role>_<pid>.jsonl` and a `.summary.json` (rewritten every 30 s, `clean_exit` false after a crash). - **Window barriers (provisional):** 16 window spawn points on Halcyon Labs sit in small spaces behind the walls (`lights: false` rooms in `layout.json`), each with a `Barrier` (6 planks) in the window cut. A zombie spawned there is Entering: it walks to the window, tears a plank every `EnemyData.plank_time` (1.5 s), swipes a player standing at the window, and climbs in once all planks are gone. Players repair one plank per press (0.5 s cooldown) for 10 points, capped at 500 per player per round (`EconomyRules`). Players collide with the Barrier layer, zombies and the navmesh bake do not, so the navmesh runs through windows and only the zombie's state keeps it outside. Each window has 3 attack slots 0.55 m apart; extra zombies wait at queue points 2 m back (3 lanes, picked by net id) and take the nearest slot that frees up. Once the planks are gone, slot holders vault in one at a time over 1.0 s on a fixed path while `ZombieRig.pose_vault` adds a 0.45 m hop, forward pitch and tucked legs. Not built yet: the AI doc's "lose the slot after 4 s with nothing to tear and move to another barrier" (no case for it in solo), a real sill in the window cut, hold-to-repair. Plank tear and repair now use generated sounds on scene-owned SFX players. Vents, the ground spawns and the tunnel grate have no barrier.
- **Emergency lighting (provisional):** every `CeilingLight` inside a `Level` with the power off runs at 30% brightness in red-orange with a red tube; on power-on each light switches back within 0.6 s (a ragged wave). No separate emergency fixtures or sound yet; the ambient light and fog stay the same. Lights outside a Level (look reference) are always normal.
- **Co-op, steps 1-3 of 6 (provisional, see `docs/MULTIPLAYER.md`):** trusted client (the person's choice, 2026-11-15): each PC moves its own player and claims its own hits; the server owns shared state. `Net.host/join/leave` with `--host` and `--join=addr:port` on the command line; `PlayerSpawner` gives each peer a `Player` named after its peer id (also its `net_id`), owned by that peer; `PoseSync` sends position, yaw and head pitch. Other players use the shared body model with no first-person gun; equipped appearance replication waits for its own step. The dedicated server runs one match at `/root/GameSession` with no player of its own; waves start once someone joins. Zombies (step 2): the server's `Spawner` spawns through a `MultiplayerSpawner`, and each zombie gets a `ZombieSync` (position, rotation, velocity, state, state time, vault time at 30 Hz); client copies run no AI and show the death pose when the state turns Dead, and vanish when the server frees the corpse. Step 4 below adds health and game over; saves remain pending (clients never bank XP). Losing the server returns to the main menu.

- **Co-op shooting (step 3):** owner traces send reliable hit claims to `HitClaims` under GameSession; solo and host still apply hits directly. The server checks a living sender (or a downed sidearm shooter in step 4) and zombie, valid hit zone/weapon, finite positive damage up to maximum pellet damage × head multiplier × head-damage stat × 1.5, range +2 m, and a sliding one-second per-player/weapon allowance of rpm / 60 × pellets × 1.5 + 2. Normal HitZone, Economy and Progression paths credit the claiming Player; reliable owner-only totals update its wallet/HUD, including an initial request after spawn. Trusted hit markers are instant; synced DEAD within 0.5 s of the last local hit produces the kill marker. RemoteShots relays one unreliable cosmetic packet per shot with muzzle and pellet endpoints (including misses); remote avatars show a shared muzzle flash, positional gun sound and short tracers. `net.hit_claim_rejected` warns at most once per second with peer/reason and drops the claim without disconnecting. Weapon switching is still local, so bounds use Content weapon data rather than checking held inventory. XP save/grant sync remains step 5.

- **Co-op, step 4 (see `docs/MULTIPLAYER.md`):** `PlayerStateSync` is a server-authority child of each client-owned Player. Reliable snapshots send current/max health, downed/dead state and per-player points/Serums, including an initial snapshot for late joins. Health regeneration and the 30 s bleed-out run on server physics ticks. Co-op lethal damage allows slow sidearm crawl with a temporary pistol when needed; reviving restores the previous gun without altering inventory; a standing teammate holds F within 1.5 m for 3 server-timed seconds to revive at full health and earn 50 points. Release, range or state changes cancel; rejected held requests retry once the server pose catches up. Dead players hide, lose collision and spectate until `round_started` respawns them at the map spawn with only the start pistol. The server ends the match when no connected squad member stands and reliably sends identical results to every client. Solo Second Wind and results timing stay unchanged. Med Dispensers/Serums remain personal; general client machine requests and save grants stay in later steps.
