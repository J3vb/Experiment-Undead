Top problems, in impact order, and completed changes:

1. **False mechanical depth.** Replace raised vent/port plates with recessed
   actions, real shroud openings and bored/ported muzzles. Completed across
   all nine guns; Halberd gains an open stock and feed-cover groove, Corvid a
   true thumbhole and barrel flutes, Hollis aligned chambers and curved flutes.
2. **Busy first-person finish.** Break continuous silver outlines into restrained
   wear and filter fine knurl, brush and pore channels with distance. Completed;
   metal, coating, polymer and wood remain distinct without image textures.
3. **Missing rarity treatment.** Add bilateral 2 mm inlays within existing
   material slots; colour only the strip and optic glass. Completed in the
   Common/Legendary preview; glass stays opaque and non-emissive. Common uses
   `#D9D9D2`, Legendary `#FF8A1C`, exposed metal `#8A918C`; base palettes retained.

Baseline rebuilt from `109bb6d` using Python 3.13 / bpy 5.3 before editing;
turnarounds initially rendered without approval, then rerendered with approval.
Godot 4.7.2 captures use fixed cameras/light, actual Weapon poses/FOV and live
picture-in-picture scopes. Software OpenGL limits this review to geometry and
finish; Forward+ lighting and GPU performance remain unverified. Compose with
`tools/compose_weapon_review.py`; the source captures are not exposure-adjusted.
Plates scale the 1600×900 frames to 800 px columns; judge pixel-distance reads
from the full-resolution captures.
The [original gear-booth companion](weapons-2026-11-15/gear_booth_companion.png)
uses `--rarity all` to include Common and Legendary: its existing single-rarity
mode has a typed-array runtime error. No gear files were changed.

All nodes, parents and transforms exactly match baseline exports; existing
ScopeLens Z 0.128 and Kestrel ADS ray tests remain unchanged. The 4,000 floor,
8,000 cap, six-material limit and 1.5× rifle tests still pass without increases.
Tests cover boolean openings, bore depth, revolver mechanics, inlay bounds,
optic finish and capture poses. No checks were weakened.

Validation: 365 asset tests, the full GUT suite, 28 model/validator checks and
28 turnaround comparisons pass; whole-repository quality checks pass.

Not changed: already distinct overall silhouettes, content camera poses,
existing placeholder viewmodel arms, simplified reload/bolt animation, and
runtime loot-to-colour wiring (rarity is previewed by the review tool).
At 10 m, fine cuts correctly disappear; the existing type silhouettes remain
the main cue, so the in-game score stays 4. Characters, gear and zombies are
outside scope; their 30 m criterion does not apply to this weapon review.
