# Zombie visual review — 2026-11-15

Baseline: current-main `109bb6d`, including the skinned-zombie rebuild, freshly
built and rendered before editing. Compared with [the lineup](../images/style_lineup.jpg),
[corridor](../images/style_corridor.jpg) and rebuilt guns' silhouettes and finish.

Problems and completed changes, largest impact first:

1. **Hand orientation and contact:** corrected mirrored thumb placement and
   finger ordering, added overlapping palms, and checked both hands through
   rest, walk and attack. Crawler hands reached 13.6 cm below the floor while
   crawling and 27.6 cm during attacks; sampled full cycles now clear it by over 1 cm.
2. **Belly overwhelmed the head:** smoother outline, darker membrane and
   restrained veins preserve the pulsing Belly while the head stays lightest.
3. **Detached, inflated sleeves:** connected the coat neckline and shoulders,
   blended yoke weights, and replaced cuffs with thin, hollow torn shells.
4. **Toy-like faces and uniform surfaces:** smaller facial forms, a real mouth
   recess, matte skin, broad wear and roughness variation; fine detail fades
   with distance. All colours still originate from the prescribed hex palette.
5. **Weak outbreak story:** refined the existing blank staff badges with an inset
   and clip, added asymmetric dark red-brown decay, and retained torn cloth and
   the missing shoe. The body proportions and rig dimensions stay unchanged.

[Black silhouettes](zombies-2026-11-15/silhouettes.png) retain distinct upright,
low and round types. [2/10/30 m](zombies-2026-11-15/distance.png) and
[24-enemy horde](zombies-2026-11-15/horde.png) plates use the same 110° horizontal
FOV and fog settings before/after. At 30 m the body outline and pale head carry
all useful detail; the small projected size and fog still limit separation of
individual enemies. Badges and wounds are close-view detail.
[Common/Legendary controls](zombies-2026-11-15/gear_booth_controls.png) come from
the actual gear booth's `--rarity all` run; zombies have no rarity. Player,
gear and gun implementations are unchanged.

Evidence is composed only from actual renders, with resizing and labels outside
the captures. Blender strips show geometry/source materials; Godot captures show
the finish shader in the authored gear-booth lighting. Godot used Compatibility
with regular fog at 0.03 as an explicitly labelled substitute for volumetric fog.
Forward+ lighting and the 60 fps GPU gate remain unverified.

Regression coverage includes hollow cuff geometry, mouth depth, unchanged bone
frames/markers, thumb chirality, palm/digit contact, Crawler ground clearance,
palette/material limits and Belly's per-instance shader flag after scene packing.
No tests or checks were weakened. No budget raise, transparent Belly, extra
decorative geometry, textures or new animation system: their cost or scope was
not justified by these views. Close hands remain deliberately angular; reference
lighting and production animation are not claimed as finished.
