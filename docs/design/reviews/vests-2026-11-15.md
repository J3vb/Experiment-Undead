Top problems, in impact order, and completed changes:

1. **Intersecting, floating construction.** Baseline medium/heavy had 120/269
   overlapping body/vest triangle pairs despite passing vertex clearance.
   Contoured caps, a flared/cut-away collar base and outward carrier thickness
   now clear the body. Shoulder straps follow the suit, pass through buckles
   and join the waist. Height-based spine weights keep stacked carrier layers
   together when bent; clavicles still own the harness crests and pads.
2. **Weak type/rarity read.** Medium/heavy now have 45/65 mm padded caps and
   0.71/0.88 m shoulder spans. Trim follows real cap faces. The chest band grew
   from 22 to 52 mm; rear bands add recognition from behind. Dark `#2F393B`
   fabric and `#1C2122` webbing separate armour from the unchanged undersuit.
3. **Exposed rear slab.** A fabric plate pocket and sewn closure replace the
   pale exposed plate. Existing MOLLE, pouch stitching and four shader finishes
   remain; status pips sit above the identification band.

(`--out <directory>`), four close angles and actual 10/30 m at 110° horizontal
FOV. The body stays Common; only the vest changes rarity. Distance glow is off;
30 m uses booth fog (density 0.01). Rest and 32° spine / 35° forearm bends use
identical cameras before/after. Extra captures render natively at 2560×1440;
use a temporary review copy with that viewport size to reproduce them.

**Limits:** static 1440p supports type/rarity recognition at 30 m, with a small
light/medium margin; 720p remains weak. Combat motion and Forward+ volumetric fog
still need an in-game GPU check. Software OpenGL does not prove final lighting.
Carrier/pouch finish is simpler than the guns. More shoulder bulk would distort
proportions; shared shader changes would affect excluded asset categories.
Blender strips show baked base colours; booth images show runtime paint zones.

Validation: 361 asset tests, 6 validator tests, fresh/committed model checks,
full GUT 428/428 followed by 23 vest/gear tests after final geometry, boot,
quality/structure and turnaround tests/diffs pass. New tests cover closed caps,
physical thickness, rear bands, connected harness, coherent skinning and full
triangle intersections/containment against the unchanged body at rest and bent.
